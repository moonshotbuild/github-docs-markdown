# Managing the GitHub Copilot plan for your enterprise

Enterprise owners can manage the Copilot plan for the enterprise.

## Links

* [Subscribing to GitHub Copilot for your enterprise](/en/copilot/how-tos/administer-copilot/manage-for-enterprise/manage-plan/subscribe)

  Enterprise owners can set up a Copilot Enterprise or Copilot Business plan for their enterprise.

* [Canceling GitHub Copilot for your enterprise](/en/copilot/how-tos/administer-copilot/manage-for-enterprise/manage-plan/cancel-plan)

  Disabling GitHub Copilot for all organizations in your enterprise will cancel your enterprise's Copilot plan.

* [Upgrading GitHub Copilot for your enterprise](/en/copilot/how-tos/administer-copilot/manage-for-enterprise/manage-plan/upgrade-plan)

  Change your organizations' plans from Copilot Business to Copilot Enterprise.

* [Downgrading GitHub Copilot for your enterprise](/en/copilot/how-tos/administer-copilot/manage-for-enterprise/manage-plan/downgrade-subscription)

  You can downgrade organizations in your enterprise from Copilot Enterprise to Copilot Business.
